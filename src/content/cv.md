This is where to place your CV/Resume!
The important part about this page is that you probably should provide a plain text
version of your CV, or at the very least the highlights of your CV. If someone
comes across your CV and wants to navigated to related websites, they may find it
easier to do so on a webpage rather than a PDF so include a list of linked material
on this webpage for convience if necessary. If you are technically focused, it may
also be worth linking to the software and tools that you fill comfortable with, it
also helps out the software having more webpages linking to them!

I personally use [Typst](https://typst.app) to create my CV as I like the functionality
when building a PDF. You may feel more comfortable however using something like LaTeX
or Word. My [academy-resume](https://github.com/angus-forrest-uk/academy-resume)
project provides a template for academic CV's (it is a WIP). To include your own
CV, copy your PDF to `public/cv.pdf` where the website can build it into the final site.
When the CV exists, the download link to the PDF appears on this webpage.
